Account Deletion

Pico Money — delete your account and associated data

Delete some of your data (without deleting your account)

You can remove individual items from your budget without deleting your account:

  • Go to the Home or History tab.
  • Swipe or tap a transaction and choose Delete.
  • Recurring bills and income items can be removed from the plan section.

These deletions are applied to your account and synced data. If you need help removing specific data, email us at jameskulu55@gmail.com.

How to delete your account

You can delete your Pico Money account and all associated data in two ways:

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  • Open Pico Money on your device.
  • Go to the Profile tab.
  • Tap Delete account at the bottom of the settings list.
  • Confirm the deletion. Your account and synced data are removed immediately.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot access the app, send a deletion request to us at:

Request account deletion by email

Include the email address you used to sign in so we can verify the request. We will process it within 30 days and confirm once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email).
  • All synced budget data: transactions, income, recurring bills, savings goals, and preferences.
Note about subscriptions: deleting your Pico Money account does not cancel a paid subscription through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. To stop future charges, cancel the subscription from your device settings (Play Store / App Store → Subscriptions).

Data retention

Once your deletion request is processed, your personal and financial data is removed from our servers. Aggregated, anonymized analytics may be retained for reporting and service improvement, as described in our Privacy Policy.
