Pico Money Privacy Policy

Your money is personal. Here's exactly what we do with your data.

1. Overview

Pico Money ("the App", "we", "us") is a budget planning app that helps you track income, bills, savings, and everyday spending. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, why we collect it, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Last updated: August 2026

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to make the App work. We do not sell your personal data.

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Financial information: The amounts you record — income, expenses, recurring bills, savings goals, and payday dates — along with categories and notes you attach to them.
  • Preferences: Your chosen currency, theme, notification, haptics, and biometric settings.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage analytics: Anonymous app events (e.g. screens viewed, features used, onboarding completion, transaction counts) via Google Analytics for Firebase. This does not include your transaction amounts or notes.
  • Subscription / purchase status: Whether a subscription is active or expired (not your payment card details — payments are handled by Google Play / Apple App Store).
  • Device & technical data: Device type, operating system, app version, and crash diagnostics, where provided by Google Analytics for Firebase.

2.3 Voice input

  • With your permission, the App uses the microphone so you can log a transaction by speaking (e.g. "spent 45 on groceries").
  • Audio is processed for speech recognition and converted to text. We do not store or listen to your recordings.

2.4 Notifications

  • If you enable reminders, the App requests permission to send notifications. Notification content is generated locally on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's core features: budget calculations, safe-to-spend guidance, transaction tracking, and insights.
  • To sync your data across your own devices when you are signed in.
  • To process and confirm your subscription status.
  • To send reminders you have opted into.
  • To improve the App through aggregated, anonymous analytics.
  • To respond to support requests and to enforce our terms.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • On your device: Most of your budget data is stored locally on your device to work offline.
  • In the cloud: When you sign in, your plan and transactions are synced securely to our backend (Supabase) so they stay safe if you lose your device.
  • Encryption in transit: Data sent to and from our servers is protected with industry-standard TLS encryption.
  • Access controls: Only you (via your sign-in) can read the financial data tied to your account. We cannot view your password, and we never store payment card details.
We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

6. Analytics & Advertising

  • The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand aggregate usage and improve features.
  • The App is ad-free. We do not show third-party ads and we do not share your data with advertising networks.
  • Analytics events do not include your transaction amounts, notes, or categories.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & correction: You can view and edit your budget data and profile information inside the App.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual transactions at any time, or delete your entire account (which removes your account and synced data) from the Profile screen.
  • Notifications & microphone: You can revoke these permissions anytime in your device Settings.
  • Analytics opt-out: You may restrict advertising identifiers or analytics via your device settings.
  • GDPR / CCPA rights: If you are in the EU/EEA/UK or California, you may have additional rights to access, port, or erase your personal data. Contact us below to exercise them.

9. Data Retention

We keep your synced data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal and financial data is removed from our servers. Analytics data is retained in aggregated, anonymized form for as long as needed for reporting and improvement.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where required, notify you in the App. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
